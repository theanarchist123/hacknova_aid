### 1. **Language Selection Button** 🌐
- **Location**: Top-right of Disaster Alerts screen (next to notifications)
- **Icon**: Language/globe icon
- **Action**: Tap to see language selection popup

### 2. **Language Selection Popup** 📱
- **Native Scripts**: हिंदी, मराठी, ગુજરાતી, தமிழ், తెలుగు, etc.

- **Same Data Source**: Both main screen and notifications use identical alert data
- **Consistent Translation**: Alerts show in your selected language everywhere
- **Native Content**: 
  - Hindi: "मुंबई के पास भूकंप" instead of "Earthquake near Mumbai"
  - Marathi: "मुंबईजवळ भूकंप" for Maharashtra users

- **Expandable Cards**: Tap "Safety Precautions" to see detailed instructions
- **Language-Specific**: 
  - English: "Drop, Cover, and Hold On"

  - Marathi: "झुकवा, लपवा आणि धरा"

## 🔧 **How to Test It:**

### Step 1: Open Disaster Alerts Screen
1. Navigate to the main disaster alerts page
2. Look for the **language icon** (🌐) in the top-right corner

### Step 2: Select Your Language
1. Tap the language icon
2. Choose your preferred language from the popup
3. See the confirmation message
4. Notice alerts are now in your selected language

### Step 3: View Translated Content
1. Scroll through the alert cards
2. Tap "Safety Precautions" to expand safety instructions
3. Notice everything is in your selected language

### Step 4: Check Notifications
1. Notifications now use the same alerts as the main screen
2. They're automatically translated to your preferred language
3. High-priority alerts (severe/critical) will trigger notifications
